TLX on sm100 (Blackwell)

Supported ops and extension builtins on NVIDIA Blackwell.

B200-class GPUs: TMA descriptors with multicast, tcgen05 async MMA with TMEM accumulators and scales, 2-CTA clusters, Cluster Launch Control (CLC), warp specialization with async_task regions, and microscaling (FP4/FP8) datatypes. Tags follow tlx.md.

Notes

•
TMA descriptors need 16-byte-aligned row strides and a trailing

stride of 1; the mm/flash_attn catalog entries enforce this via accepts.

•
packed_add4/sub4/mul4/fma4 target sm103+ PTX and are unconfirmed

here (?).

•
nv_mma_layout / nv_mma_shared_layout_encoding are Hopper WGMMA

layouts; unconfirmed on Blackwell (?).
