TLX on gfx1250 (RDNA4)

TDM descriptors and expected support on next-gen AMD.

Next-generation AMD target. The distinguishing TLX feature is TDM (Tensor Descriptor Machine): hardware descriptors with fused multi-member loads, multicast, and prefetch, mirroring what TMA provides on NVIDIA. Tags follow tlx.md; a trailing ? marks availability that still needs verification — most of this page is ? by design.

Supported tlx.ops

No tlx.ops catalog entries target gfx1250 yet. The closest coverage is the tutorial third_party/tlx/tutorials/amd_grouped_gemm_gfx1250/, which exercises fused TDM loads/stores, TDM prefetch, amd_sched_barrier, and amd_set_wave_sched_mode in a grouped-GEMM experiment (see its README).

Notes

•
Promoting a gfx1250 tutorial into tlx.ops means adding a catalog entry

with arch="gfx1250"; see tlx-ops for the process.

•
Fused TDM loads require disjoint warp masks and support at most 5

multicast recipients; async_amd_descriptor_wait takes pendings xor tokens, not both.
