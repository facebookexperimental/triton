TLX on gfx950 (MI350)

Supported ops and extension builtins on AMD MI350.

MI350X/MI355X: 256 CUs in 8 XCDs (32 CUs each), 160 KB LDS per CU, CDNA4 MFMA with 32-row tiles, direct HBM-to-LDS async copies (buffer_load_to_lds), in-LDS transpose reads, and inter-wave scheduling control. Tags follow tlx.md: gfx950 means confirmed on this target.

Notes

•
buffer_load_to_local needs 32/128-bit width plus an aligned mask;

f16/bf16 buffer_atomic_add needs width 2 plus a pair-uniform mask.

•
amd_scheduled_mfma distinguishes transient vs persistent accumulator

chains; persistent dV stays in AGPRs while dK stays in VGPRs in the tuned backward paths.

•
TDM descriptor ops (async_amd_descriptor_*) are gfx1250+, not gfx950;

see TLX on gfx1250.
